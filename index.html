NO MYSTERY MATH

One month. Every credit accounted for.

We multiply your daily messages by the days in your billing month, then by each plan’s credits per message. Any shortfall is covered with whole top-up packs, rounded up. Your estimate is the subscription fee plus those packs.

Use the same currency for every plan (the display is USD). For plans billed yearly, enter the monthly equivalent; your upfront payment will differ. Estimates exclude tax, discounts, credit rollover, daily resets, voice and image use, and changing model rates. “Unlimited” assumes no extra message charge; fair-use rules may still apply. A lower cost isn’t a quality ranking.

What if a platform uses tokens or daily allowances?

LLM tokens are not fixed-price message credits. This calculator assumes a fixed credit charge per sent message and a monthly allowance. Use a representative average only if you understand the variation. It does not model daily credit expiry, context-window costs, or variable token billing. For those plans, the result may not reflect actual spend.
